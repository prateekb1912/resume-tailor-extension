Privacy Policy for Tailr

Last updated: September 7, 2026

  1. Overview

    Tailr ("the extension") is a Chrome browser extension, with a companion web dashboard, that helps you tailor your resume to job descriptions. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

  2. Data we collect

    Resume data — your resume content (work experience, skills, education, contact info) that you upload to the extension.

    Job description text — the text of job listings you choose to tailor your resume against, extracted from the page you are currently viewing.

    Account information — your email address and password (stored as a salted hash), used to authenticate you with the Tailr service.

    Job preferences — if you use the web dashboard, search preferences (target titles, locations, seniority, exclusions) you set to match job listings against your profile.

  3. How we use your data

    Your resume and job description data are sent to our backend server (hosted at tailr-api.onrender.com) to generate a tailored version of your resume and a job-fit score. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, or for any purpose beyond providing the tailoring and matching service.

  4. Data storage

    Your resume profile and preferences are stored on our backend, associated with your account, so they persist across sessions and are available to both the extension and the web dashboard. The extension also caches your session token, resume profile, and pending tailoring results locally in Chrome's storage API, so the popup works without constantly re-fetching data; this local cache is cleared when you sign out.

  5. Third-party AI providers

    Resume parsing, resume tailoring, and job-fit scoring are performed using large language model APIs (OpenAI and/or Anthropic). Your resume content and job description text are sent to these providers solely to generate that output — they are not used by us to train models, and their own retention is governed by their respective privacy policies.

  6. Data sharing

    We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. Resume and job description content is transmitted only between your browser, the Tailr backend (operated solely by us), and the AI providers described above.

  7. Data security

    All communication between the extension, the dashboard, and our backend is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

  8. Your rights

    You can delete your resume profile at any time from the dashboard. You can clear all locally cached data by signing out or removing the extension from Chrome. To request full account deletion, contact us below.

  9. Contact

    Questions about this policy or your data? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
